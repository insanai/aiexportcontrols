CHINA / ANNOUNCEMENT 48

Registration now.
A new catalogue proposed.

Since 16 October 2026, covered technology exports require temporary registration, including unfinished transfers. The proposed list covers AI, biomedicine and solar technology. Consultation closes on 15 November.

For AI: the draft excludes already public technology and proposes easier licensing for new open-source exports of restricted technology. It is not a blanket ban on open models.

UNITED STATES / AI CONTROLS

Chips need permission.
Model rules need a closer read.

Advanced chips face destination, buyer and use checks. Since 15 January 2026, qualifying H200-class exports from the US to China or Macau can receive individual review under conditions. Approval is not automatic.

For AI weights: the current text still includes the AI Diffusion Rule's model entry. BIS announced non-enforcement, while GAO says that announcement did not formally repeal the rule and raised a procedural problem.
