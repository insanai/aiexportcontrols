Editorial and visual review
===========================

Review date: 2 November 2026.

The book's strongest argument is that broad restrictions can damage shared research and leave both countries worse off. We retain that argument and make its conditions explicit. We distinguish official rules, proposed changes, hypothetical examples and policy recommendations.

Substantive corrections
-----------------------

* We removed claims that invented game scores measured a 70% innovation loss or a fourfold safety risk. The game now explains a possible Prisoner's Dilemma under stated preferences. It also explains when security benefits or unequal capabilities could change those preferences.
* We distinguish a Nash equilibrium from collective benefit and Pareto efficiency. We show the repeated-game calculation under explicit assumptions. The independent-review probability example describes undetected flaws, rather than claiming to measure overall AI safety.
* We separate China's current temporary registration from the proposed catalogue. We distinguish an exclusion from proposed automatic licensing and avoid promising an approval time.
* We replace the universal before-and-after release story with separate questions about public files, first releases, private derivatives, Chinese requirements and US jurisdiction. An open copyright license does not settle every other obligation.
* We correct 266506X to two main points. Memory designs have alternative characteristics; 18 nm is not a universal threshold for every memory technology. Cluster coverage concerns specified technology for more than 10,000 accelerator cards.
* We clarify that a license cannot authorize a prohibited solar-technology transfer. Product exclusions and remote-service explanations are limited to their stated scope.
* We describe GAO Decision B-337935 as a decision about Congressional Review Act submission. We separately verify the published 4E091 text and describe BIS's announced non-enforcement policy.
* We replace inert source labels with native hyperlinks that work in HTML and PDF. We add NTIA's report, open-model risk research, an authors' game-theory textbook and the original Jefferson letter. The manifest now records 26 sources, with hashes for 24 downloaded archives and explicit limitations for the other two.

Evidence anchors
----------------

* `MOFCOM Announcement 48 <https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2026/art_0a31a3c9ac404f7a9b0e917ce3412997.html>`_ and its preserved attachments define the Chinese announcement and draft.
* `BIS Part 734 <https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/734>`_, `Part 774 <https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774>`_ and `GAO B-337935 <https://www.gao.gov/products/b-337935>`_ support the separate US treatment.
* `NTIA's 2024 open-model report <https://www.ntia.gov/sites/default/files/publications/ntia-ai-open-model-report.pdf>`_ and `On the Societal Impact of Open Foundation Models <https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07918>`_ support a balanced discussion of access and risk. Neither supplies a universal safety multiplier.
* `Pass and Tseng's textbook <https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~rafael/networks-html/chapter1.html>`_ supports the strategic concepts. Our numerical scores remain our teaching assumptions.

Visual changes
--------------

We replace the active raster illustrations with original native Typst and CeTZ line drawings. Fine dark lines carry structure. Teal marks exchange or continuity. Red marks interruption. Shape and captions also convey these meanings, so color is not the only cue. We remove textures, dramatic lighting, gradients and ornamental framing from these illustrations.

The drawings cover shared knowledge, computing dependencies, release paths, medicine platforms and the three technology sectors. The teaching matrix uses a restrained table with explicit assumption labels. The same source modules supply the website and PDF, without a separate image-generation step. Earlier raster assets remain unused in the repository.

Validation
----------

We build through the native GuideDog 1.0.0-beta.10 pipeline. All seven HTML and PDF editions build with warnings treated as errors. The six translated catalog sets cover the current extracted source messages with no missing, empty or fuzzy entries. Catalog coverage does not establish professional translation accuracy.

We check the assembled site's internal links and anchors, archive hashes, PDF text, required control codes and credits. Browser checks exercise all seven editions, the 40-card atlas and its sector, status and code filters. We also check live search, the scenario selector, language links, desktop and mobile layouts, dark mode, and image loading.

The final assembly contains 99 HTML pages and 5,166 checked internal links, with no missing files or anchors. The seven PDF editions contain 40 to 48 pages. Poppler emits a tagging-metadata diagnostic about the Suspects field during text extraction, which completes successfully. A separate MuPDF check verifies author metadata and renders the cover and colophon in every edition.

This review records a local build and source review. It does not establish the outcome of a future regulatory decision or clear an individual transaction. The published date on a source, a contact detail and a current legal rule are separate facts that can change independently.
