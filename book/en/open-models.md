A model can have downloadable weights, an open software license, and enough published training information to reproduce it. These are different properties. The Open Source Initiative's definition asks for freedoms to use, study, change and share, along with data information, code and parameters. A model described casually as open source does not automatically satisfy that definition. That definition is also not the Chinese regulator's legal test. [OSI](https://opensource.org/ai/open-source-ai-definition)

DeepSeek's R1 repository says its code and model weights use the MIT license and identifies additional ancestry details for distilled versions. Qwen's Qwen3 repository documents downloadable models and an Apache 2.0 license. These are concrete examples of existing public releases, not proof of the status of every model from either company or of any future release. [R1](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1) [QWEN](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3)

## Already public and about to become public

The AI section's first footnote excludes technology already in the public domain through open source or academic publication. Its second footnote offers automatic licensing or similar facilitation for open-source exports of restricted AI technology. The distinction is meaningful: an existing public artifact and a first release of private technology need not follow the same route. [CN-A1, notes 1 and 2](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

Publishing a paper about a model does not necessarily publish its private weights or training code. We should identify the particular artifact and what was actually made available. This is our reading of a technology-specific exclusion. The announcement does not resolve every question about gated downloads, a partly open release or later private improvements. [CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

## Four possible paths, not four predictions

**Continued open releases.** Easier licensing works as described, and firms keep releasing models because broad use is valuable. Evidence supporting this possibility is the express open-source facilitation policy. Evidence that would weaken it includes new rules narrowing that policy or documented delays. [CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

**More preparation before release.** Teams document what they publish, which data they used and which threshold applies. This is our inference from the alternative tests and the distinction between public and newly released technology. It does not prove that releases will slow. [CN-A1, 266504X](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

**A divided product line.** A company releases one model publicly while keeping a more advanced version, special dataset or agent system private. This is a business possibility, not a requirement in the announcement. It follows from controls attaching to specific technology and transfer forms. [CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

**More hosted access.** Some providers choose services instead of transferring core technology to customers. MOFCOM's service clarification makes that plausible for restricted large models. It does not guarantee price, continued service, privacy protection or immunity from data-related prohibitions. [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

## What would change our view

We would watch the final catalogue, definitions of public release, the practical automatic-licensing procedure, treatment of new checkpoints and derivatives, and export-time ranking methods. We would also watch access to computing hardware under US rules. No source here supports a numerical probability that Chinese open models will disappear, or a date when they will stop. We assign neither. [CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [EAR742](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/742) [EAR744](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744)

For an overseas developer, an existing permissive license is useful evidence of reuse rights. It is not a promise of future versions or a general export-law clearance. A practical record contains the exact release, license, download date and changes made later. That is our documentation suggestion, not an added legal duty.
