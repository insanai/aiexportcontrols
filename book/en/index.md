**Research edition: 2 November 2026.** We separate rules already operating, proposals still open for comment, and our own analysis. This is a public explanation, not a ruling on a particular transaction.

## Two countries. Different gates.

**CHINA | Registration now, catalogue changes proposed.** The 16 October announcement opens consultation on 40 technologies: 23 in AI, 5 in biomedicine and 12 in solar. Export registration starts immediately for covered transfers, including unfinished ones. The proposed list contains 14 prohibited and 26 restricted technologies. The comment period ends on 15 November 2026. [CN48](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2026/art_0a31a3c9ac404f7a9b0e917ce3412997.html) [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

**UNITED STATES | Chip controls operate, model-rule status needs care.** Advanced AI chips face license requirements and checks on their destination, buyer and use. Since 15 January 2026, qualifying H200-class exports from the US to China or Macau can receive individual review under conditions. That is permission to apply, not automatic permission to ship. The AI Diffusion Rule remains in the current published rules, while BIS has announced non-enforcement. GAO challenged the procedure for that policy in May 2026. [US26](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/html/2026-00789.htm) [EAR742](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/742) [USNE](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens) [GAO26](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-337935) [EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774)

## The central finding

The announcement does not establish a blanket ban on Chinese open models. Already public AI technology is excluded in the draft. New open-source exports of restricted AI technology are offered automatic licensing or similar facilitation. An exclusion and a permission procedure are different things. Neither erases other laws. [CN48](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2026/art_0a31a3c9ac404f7a9b0e917ce3412997.html) [CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)
