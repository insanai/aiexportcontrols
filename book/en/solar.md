# 06. Solar: knowledge can travel inside a machine

## Three stages of production

The solar section covers making polysilicon, turning silicon into wafers and making solar cells. Proposed prohibitions include specified high-capacity polysilicon processes, wafer technology at 70 micrometers or thinner, specified edge-passivation technology and specified perovskite or tandem-cell manufacturing. These are process and equipment boundaries, not a blanket ban on finished solar panels. ## The equipment exception to a simple story

Attachment 1's fourth footnote expressly includes key equipment carrying the relevant technology. It names categories such as specified reactors, wafer-cutting machines, crystal-growing furnaces, coating tools and cell-processing equipment. MOFCOM says the equipment provision concerns machines carrying technology that meets the control indicators; equipment not named is generally outside this announcement's requirement. [CN-A1, note 4](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps) [CNQA, solar question](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

Selling a finished panel, sharing factory drawings and installing a production line are different activities. Installation, debugging, improvement and upgrades can transfer know-how as well as machinery. The official explanation discusses licensing procedures for covered technology transfers and temporary registration for the listed key equipment. We retain the distinction between the proposal and registration already in place. [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)

## A factory example

Suppose a supplier sends an overseas buyer an entire cell-production line with operating software and training. We identify each machine, its performance and the knowledge being delivered. We then check the equipment footnote, technology points, transfer form and current registration duty. Treating the delivery as only hardware would miss part of the announced scope. ## Trade policy is a different layer

Tariffs change the cost of imports. Export controls regulate whether or how an outward transfer may occur. Neither word can substitute for the other. The US comparison in this book focuses on technology and AI controls; it does not turn solar tariff disputes into equivalent technology bans. A controlled manufacturing recipe can affect overseas factory plans without implying that every resulting product is controlled. This is our analysis of the technology/product distinction in the official explanation. [CNQA](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html) [EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774)
