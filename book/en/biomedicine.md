# 05. Biomedicine: platforms, targets and cooperation

## The reusable engine and the individual medicine

The proposed prohibited entries include Chinese-owned original cell and gene therapy platforms and siRNA drug platforms. A platform here is reusable underlying technology that can support several drugs. The notes limit these prohibitions to overseas transfers of ownership. Licensing and contract research or production without an ownership transfer are outside that platform scope. Technology for a single drug is also distinguished from the reusable platform. [CN-A1, 262704J and 262705J](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

The notes also exclude technology needed for development, trials, production, quality control, marketing and compliance in cooperation or business deals involving particular medicines at different stages. We should check the actual subject of a deal. A transaction described as a license can still raise questions if its substance transfers the platform's ownership. That is an inference from the ownership boundary. ## New targets have a different route

The restricted entries concern discovery and validation of wholly new targets for cell and gene therapy and siRNA drugs. The target must not have been publicly disclosed worldwide through the named routes, including patents, papers, public databases, technical materials and conferences. A promising drug is not the same thing as a wholly new undisclosed target. [CN-A1, 262705X and 262706X](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

The biomedicine restricted-section footnote offers automatic licensing or similar facilitation for public-health emergencies, humanitarian help, joint research using overseas resources and overseas licensing. We cannot carry that footnote over to every proposed prohibited technology. [CN-A1, note 3](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

## The biosecurity entry

The pathogen entry concerns specified enhancement and directed-passage technology. We describe its legal subject at a high level rather than giving laboratory methods. It does not establish a blanket ban on medical research or ordinary medicines. [CN-A1, 267302J](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

## How the US comparison helps

The US Commerce Control List has biological agents, equipment and related technology entries, and EAR 744.4 addresses chemical and biological weapons end uses. That structure is not a direct equivalent of China's proposed protection of reusable drug platforms. The US Data Security Program separately controls certain access to sensitive personal and government-related data. A genomics project can therefore raise data questions as well as technology questions. [EAR774, Category 1](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774) [EAR744, 744.4](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744) [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/nsd/data-security)

## An example, with limits

A company licenses rights for one named drug abroad while keeping ownership of its reusable platform. The platform notes are directly relevant, and the new-target provisions may require a separate check. We cannot treat all technology inside one contract as a single undifferentiated object. This example illustrates how to read the categories; it does not approve a contract.
