# 05. 生物医药：平台、靶点与合作

## 可复用平台和单一药物

拟禁止条目包括具有自主知识产权的细胞和基因治疗平台以及siRNA药物平台。平台是可用于开发多款药物的底层通用技术。注释仅禁止所有权向境外转让；不转让所有权的许可、外包研发和生产等不在该平台管理范围。单一药物开发技术也有区别。[CN-A1，262704J、262705J](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

与特定药物合作交易相关、为研发、试验、生产、质控、上市及合规所必需的技术亦有排除。合同名称不足以决定其内容；如果实际转移平台所有权，仍需重新判断。这是我们对所有权边界的分析。[CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

## 新靶点有另一条路径

限制类针对细胞和基因治疗及siRNA药物全新靶点的发现和验证技术。全新意味着在全球范围内未通过专利、论文、数据库、技术资料、会议等公开披露。一个有前景的药物并不等于尚未公开的新靶点。[CN-A1，262705X、262706X](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

公共卫生紧急事件、人道主义援助、利用境外资源联合研发以及对外授权等，可适用限制类生物医药技术的自动许可等便利措施。这条注释不能自动覆盖全部禁止类技术。[CN-A1，注3](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

## 生物安全与美国对照

病原微生物条目针对规定的增强及定向传代技术，不是所有医学研究。我们只介绍法律范围，不提供实验方法。[CN-A1，267302J](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)

美国商业管制清单包含生物因子、设备和相关技术，EAR744.4涉及化学及生物武器最终用途。它与中国对可复用药物平台的拟管制并非一一对应。美国数据安全计划另涉及敏感个人及政府相关数据，因此基因项目也可能涉及数据问题。[EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774) [EAR744](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744) [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/nsd/data-security)

例如，一家公司只许可一个药物，保留平台所有权。我们需要分别检查平台注释和新靶点条目，不能把合同内所有技术视为一个对象。这个例子不构成交易批准。[CN-A1](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)
