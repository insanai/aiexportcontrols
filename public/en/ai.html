03. AI: what is actually covered¶

A model is more than a chat window¶

Weights are learned numbers that make a model work. Source code tells a computer how to build or run it. Checkpoints are saved stages of training. Architecture is the design. A hosted service lets us ask questions without receiving all those parts. Attachment 1 names weights, key checkpoints, underlying code, architecture, training improvement and faster model operation in its advanced-model entry. CN-A1, 266504X(2)

Three alternative tests for advanced models¶

Proposed entry 266504X(2) covers core technology of a large model meeting any one of three tests: training calculation of at least 10^26 floating-point operations, at least 2 trillion parameters, or globally leading ability. The last test means a top-10 public ranking at the time of export across the kinds of assessments named in the draft, including overall or specialist ability, use, safety and training efficiency. It is not confined to one popular chat leaderboard. CN-A1, 266504X(2), note 3

The parameter test and calculation test are alternatives, not a pair that must both be met. A smaller model could qualify through the ranking test. A claim that a named model crosses a threshold needs evidence about that exact version and the time of export. We do not assign legal classifications to DeepSeek or Qwen from their brand names. CN-A1

Data creates a separate boundary¶

Models trained on China's core data and then provided as services appear in the proposed prohibited section, 266501J. Models trained on important data and then provided as services appear in restricted entry 266504X(1). Core and important data are legal categories determined by the Data Security Law and related rules, not everyday labels for valuable data. CN-A1, 266501J and 266504X

The important-data entry has a qualified exclusion. One route concerns a trace share of at most 0.1%, with further conditions about technical capability, cleaning and preventing reproduction in outputs. Another concerns data that can lawfully be obtained publicly. A small percentage alone is not a safe conclusion. The exact structure of the Chinese note must be checked. CN-A1, 266504X, note 1

Agents, chips and the system around the model¶

Agents can break a request into tasks, use tools or coordinate other agents. The draft names four restricted capabilities with performance conditions. An agent used mainly in highly sensitive settings can be proposed prohibited when it meets the referenced performance criteria. General-purpose agents are not all placed in that category merely because they can act. CN-A1, 266502J and 266505X

AI chip design and memory chip design have their own proposed restrictions. The processor-design entry uses alternative characteristics, some broadly worded, including floating-point or integer capability and specified applications. It should not be summarized as a 14 nm-only rule. The footnote offers facilitation for overseas chip fabrication; the announcement does not establish a general ban on all foreign foundry work. CN-A1, 266506X and note 2

Cluster entries concern systems with more than 10,000 AI accelerator cards. They cover fast links, recovery after failures, communication scheduling and mixed-vendor computing, subject to their listed tests. The target includes the knowledge that keeps a huge computer installation working, not only the model inside it. CN-A1, 266507X

Overseas deployment and remote access¶

MOFCOM says overseas local deployment of restricted model technology falls within technology-export management. An overseas cloud owned by the Chinese business may qualify for easier licensing. Its answer on restricted large models places API access, apps, web interaction, SaaS and lightweight local deployment outside large-model technology-export management. That answer should not be extended automatically to prohibited core-data technology, private weights handed to a partner, or every other law. CNQA, overseas deployment question
