## How to use the atlas

We read the short description to understand the subject, then open the attachment at the stated code. A number alone is rarely sufficient. Some entries require every condition, some require any one condition, and some apply only to a special purpose or setting. The summaries preserve the main boundary but do not reproduce every measurement condition. The original Chinese text governs.

Public-domain AI exclusions and the footnotes on easier licensing apply across relevant entries. Solar also covers specified equipment that carries the technology. Biomedicine distinguishes reusable platforms from work on individual drugs. These notes are part of the substance, not optional small print. [CN-A1, notes 1 to 4](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6KYc7i4iYCs13RxyX1cYsF7Mi050tv2zWwJDAqyqUOFf3lPQeAoPgP%2BrmQMGF7Tta7RdRW1C2hni5Ufl7eigFU2aoR9BIV7bpVR%2BwDBnd46wm5m7QVY2Mj%2FXPJaIbSdBzxKM6WqPb1yMuUzPhfLWixM%3D&fileName=1.%E6%8B%9F%E5%88%97%E5%85%A5%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E7%A6%81%E6%AD%A2%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E5%87%BA%E5%8F%A3%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E7%9B%AE%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E3%80%81%E7%94%9F%E7%89%A9%E5%8C%BB%E8%8D%AF%E3%80%81%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%8C%83%E5%9B%B4%EF%BC%88%E5%BE%81%E6%B1%82%E6%84%8F%E8%A7%81%E7%A8%BF%EF%BC%89.wps)
