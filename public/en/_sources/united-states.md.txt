# 07. America's controls, in plain language

## Four questions before an answer

In the US system we ask whether the item falls under the Export Administration Regulations, how it is classified, where it is going, and who will use it for what. US-origin items and some foreign-made items can be covered. The foreign-made rules have specific conditions; a vague connection to America does not by itself settle jurisdiction. [EAR734, 734.3 and 734.9](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/734) [EAR744](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744)

The Commerce Control List assigns classification codes. Advanced computing chips are addressed in entries including 3A090. End-user and end-use rules can create requirements beyond the basic destination table. The Entity List is one part of this system. Inclusion and license conditions must be checked for the actual parties, rather than inferred from a country label. [EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774) [EAR744, 744.11 and 744.23](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744)

## The January 2026 chip change

The 15 January final rule permits case-by-case review for qualifying exports from the US to end users in China or Macau. The stated performance limits are total processing performance below 21,000 and total DRAM bandwidth below 6,500 GB/s. H200 and MI325X are examples, not a substitute for classifying the exact product. [US26](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/html/2026-00789.htm) [EAR742, 742.6(b)(10)](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/742)

Conditions include adequate US supply, no diversion of relevant foundry capacity from US users, a limit relating shipments to China and Macau to US shipments, customer checks, security measures and independent testing in the US. Approval remains discretionary. The rule does not give the same favorable policy to every reexport or shipment to a China-linked company in another country. [US26, sections I and IV](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/html/2026-00789.htm)

## The AI Diffusion Rule: written law and enforcement

The January 2025 framework introduced controls on certain advanced model weights and broader computing exports. Current BIS-published text still includes 4E091 for model parameters trained using at least 10^26 operations. Its notes exclude published parameters and provide qualified treatment for derivatives and models below an open-model capability reference. The full notes matter. [US25](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-15/html/2025-00636.htm) [EAR774, 4E091](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774)

On 13 May 2025, BIS announced non-enforcement and a planned rescission. On 12 May 2026, GAO said the rule remained legally in effect pending formal rulemaking and concluded that the blanket non-enforcement policy itself required submission under the Congressional Review Act. The current text checked for this book still contains the model-weight entry. We therefore do not describe the rule as simply repealed, or portray its licensing provisions as straightforwardly enforced. A press release, a rulebook and a legal oversight decision answer different questions. [USNE](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens) [GAO26](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-337935) [EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774)

## Public information has an important place

EAR 734.7 generally treats qualifying published, unclassified technology and software as outside the EAR, with stated exceptions. EAR 734.8 addresses results of fundamental research intended for publication. Published model parameters are expressly addressed in 4E091. These provisions do not make every private checkpoint, encryption tool or restricted release exempt just because a related paper exists. [EAR734](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/734) [EAR774](https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/774)

## Nearby rules are not interchangeable

Treasury's outbound investment program covers certain US investments involving semiconductors, quantum technology and AI in relation to China, Hong Kong and Macau. The 2024 final rule took effect on 2 January 2025. Treasury issued a further proposal on 22 October 2026, with comments due on 7 December. A new proposal is not automatically a new final investment rule. [TREAS](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/international/outbound-investment-program)

The Justice Department's Data Security Program concerns specified access to sensitive data. These programs can overlap with an AI project, but neither is the same as an export license for a chip or a model. We label them separately to avoid double counting or claiming that one approval answers every question. [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/nsd/data-security) [TREAS](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/international/outbound-investment-program)
