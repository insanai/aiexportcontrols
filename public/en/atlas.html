02. The 40-technology atlas¶

Count the technologies, not the table rows¶

The official headline counts 40 technologies. Attachment 1 groups several technology points under the same catalogue code. It does not contain 40 separate code rows. The explorer below splits those points into 40 reading entries and retains their original code and point number. This is an editorial index, not a replacement legal list. CN48 CN-A1

AI accounts for 23 points, with 6 proposed prohibited points and 17 restricted points. Biomedicine has 3 prohibited and 2 restricted points. Solar has 5 prohibited and 7 restricted points. Those add to the official totals of 14 and 26. The sector breakdown by status is our count of Attachment 1's numbered points. CN-A1 CNQA

How to use the atlas¶

We read the short description to understand the subject, then open the attachment at the stated code. A number alone is rarely sufficient. Some entries require every condition, some require any one condition, and some apply only to a special purpose or setting. The summaries preserve the main boundary but do not reproduce every measurement condition. The original Chinese text governs.

Public-domain AI exclusions and the footnotes on easier licensing apply across relevant entries. Solar also covers specified equipment that carries the technology. Biomedicine distinguishes reusable platforms from work on individual drugs. These notes are part of the substance, not optional small print. CN-A1, notes 1 to 4

01. Sensitive deepfake detection¶

266401J / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Detection technology mainly for China's cross-border network-data security, with both accuracy and recall at least 95%. The specified purpose is part of the boundary. CN-A1

02. AI-driven malicious code¶

266402J / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Specified malicious-code generation or mutation usable against highly sensitive systems or national defenses, meeting one of the listed attack-success tests. This is not every coding assistant. CN-A1

03. Adaptive malicious behavior¶

266402J / 2 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Specified technology for adapting in adversarial environments or changing behavior while running, with listed evasion or rewriting properties and the sensitive-use qualifier. CN-A1

04. Automated malicious variants¶

266402J / 3 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Specified automated production and distributed deployment of evading malicious variants, subject to listed scale or performance tests and the sensitive-use qualifier. CN-A1

05. Models trained on core data¶

266501J / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Large models trained using China's legally defined core data and then provided as services. The data category is determined by law, not by a model's brand. CN-A1

06. Agents for highly sensitive settings¶

266502J / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed prohibited

Agents mainly for critical infrastructure, defense or core government settings that also reach the performance criteria referenced in 266505X. CN-A1

07. High-accuracy deepfake detection¶

266402X / 8 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Deepfake detection systems and equipment-manufacturing technology with both accuracy and recall at least 98%. This adds a point to an existing category. CN-A1

08. Hidden information in images and video¶

266402X / 9 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Embedding and extracting hidden information meeting all three groups of tests: robustness, false positives at most 0.1%, and near-imperceptibility. The attachment gives alternative metrics within groups. CN-A1

09. AI-assisted vulnerability discovery¶

266404X / 6 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Tools meeting any of six listed capability tests, including benchmark scores, independent exploitation capability or specified efficiency gains. Ordinary security testing is not automatically covered. CN-A1

10. Voiceprint recognition¶

266501X / 19 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Speaker-identification technology meeting any listed recognition, response-speed, anti-spoofing or minority-language/dialect criterion. Each test has its own measurement conditions. CN-A1

11. Chinese and minority-language translation¶

266501X / 20 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Technology mainly for Chinese and minority languages meeting a listed human-rating or semantic-fidelity and BLEU test. Language purpose and test conditions matter. CN-A1

12. Models trained on important data¶

266504X / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Large models trained on China's legally defined important data and then provided as services, with qualified exclusions for trace use with safeguards or lawfully public data. CN-A1

13. Advanced large-model core technology¶

266504X / 2 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Core model technology if any test is met: at least 10^26 training calculations, at least 2 trillion parameters, or a qualifying global top-10 ranking at export time. Includes weights, checkpoints, code and architecture. CN-A1

14. Agent planning¶

266505X / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Natural-language instructions split into at least 20 executable subtasks, with decomposition accuracy at least 80% and average subtask success at least 90%. All these conditions apply. CN-A1

15. Many agents working together¶

266505X / 2 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

At least 60 concurrent agents, task time at most twice the minimum single-agent time for the same effect, and final-answer agreement at least 85%. CN-A1

16. Tool-using agents¶

266505X / 3 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

At least 200 integrated third-party tools, selection accuracy at least 90%, and average end-to-end success at least 90% in tasks requiring three or more tools. CN-A1

17. Strategic decision-making agents¶

266505X / 4 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

All three groups apply: at least 20 planning steps or 10 concurrent agents; adaptive decisions within 200 ms with at least 10 control units; and a standard game benchmark above 90% of humans. CN-A1

18. AI processor design¶

266506X / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Design meeting any listed characteristic, including 14 nm or smaller fabrication, floating-point or integer capability, advanced packaging, fast links, novel architectures or named applications. It is broader than one chip-size test. CN-A1

19. Memory chip design¶

266506X / 2 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Specified 3D flash or memory design. Alternative flash tests include density, I/O speed or bonding; memory tests include 18 nm or smaller processing, vertical channels, bonding or multilayer stacking. CN-A1

20. Fast cluster connections¶

266507X / 1 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

Inter-chip or inter-node connections with bidirectional bandwidth at least 800 GB/s in clusters with more than 10,000 AI accelerator cards. CN-A1

21. Recovery during distributed training¶

266507X / 2 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

In clusters above 10,000 cards, any listed recovery or waste criterion, such as average checkpoint recovery at most 120 seconds, visible interruption at most 30 seconds or network-related wasted compute at most 5%. CN-A1

22. Efficient cluster communication¶

266507X / 3 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

In clusters above 10,000 cards, any listed test: linear scaling at least 98%; specified routing and balancing with latency at most 1 microsecond; or communication hiding at least 90%. CN-A1

23. Mixed-vendor cluster scheduling¶

266507X / 4 | Artificial intelligence | Proposed restricted

In clusters above 10,000 cards, unified scheduling or joint inference across chips from at least two vendors with a listed protocol, efficiency, recovery or geographic-scheduling property. CN-A1

24. Cell and gene therapy platforms¶

262704J / 1 | Biomedicine | Proposed prohibited

Chinese-owned original reusable technology platforms. The prohibition is limited to transferring ownership abroad; non-ownership licensing and specified services or single-drug work are excluded. CN-A1

25. siRNA medicine platforms¶

262705J / 1 | Biomedicine | Proposed prohibited

Chinese-owned original reusable siRNA drug platforms, with the same ownership-transfer boundary and stated exclusions for licensing, services and particular drug work. CN-A1

26. Specified pathogen enhancement technology¶

267302J / 1 | Biomedicine | Proposed prohibited

Specified artificial genetic modification or directed-passage enhancement technologies with any listed characteristic. This is a biosecurity category, not all biomedical research. CN-A1

27. New cell and gene therapy targets¶

262705X / 1 | Biomedicine | Proposed restricted

Chinese-owned technology to discover and validate wholly new targets not publicly disclosed worldwide through the stated routes. Restricted-biomedicine facilitation applies to specified circumstances. CN-A1

28. New siRNA targets¶

262706X / 1 | Biomedicine | Proposed restricted

Chinese-owned discovery and validation technology for wholly new siRNA targets, using the same undisclosed-target definition and restricted-biomedicine facilitation note. CN-A1

29. High-capacity cold hydrogenation¶

263003J / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed prohibited

A cold-hydrogenation fluidized-bed process with single-line trichlorosilane capacity at least 250,000 tonnes per year, and equipment-manufacturing technology. CN-A1

30. High-capacity silane fluidized beds¶

263003J / 2 | Solar energy | Proposed prohibited

Silane fluidized-bed processes with single-line capacity at least 20,000 tonnes per year, and equipment-manufacturing technology. CN-A1

31. Ultra-thin wafers¶

263004J / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed prohibited

Technology for wafers 70 micrometers thick or thinner, including associated equipment-manufacturing technology. The note includes code, underlying algorithms and drawings. CN-A1

32. Specified cell-edge treatment¶

263801J / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed prohibited

Solar-specific edge passivation for crystalline-silicon cells, with full-wafer edge lengths 182 to 230 mm and wafer thickness at most 140 micrometers, plus equipment-manufacturing technology. CN-A1

33. Perovskite and tandem manufacturing¶

263801J / 2 | Solar energy | Proposed prohibited

Four specified routes: mass-production silicon-perovskite tandem cells at least 182 mm and 30% efficient; slot coating at least 1.1 m wide; single-junction modules at least 1 m2 and 18%; or tandem modules at least 1 m2 and 28%. CN-A1

34. Large polysilicon plant engineering¶

263004X / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Single-line polysilicon engineering capacity at least 30,000 tonnes per year. Engineering includes equipment and building designs and process packages. CN-A1

35. High-output reduction processes¶

263004X / 2 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Reduction processes with polysilicon output at least 800 tonnes per furnace per year, and equipment-manufacturing technology. CN-A1

36. Crystal-growing designs¶

263005X / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Specified solar crystal-growing designs, including chamber diameter at least 1,600 mm, thermal-field size at least 35 inches, pulling speed at least 1.2 mm/minute or equal-diameter power at most 40 kW. CN-A1

37. Silicon-ingot machining¶

263005X / 2 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Specified half-ingot cutting/grinding technology or machining technology for ingots at least 720 mm long, plus equipment-manufacturing technology. CN-A1

38. Large or thin wafer processes¶

263005X / 3 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Specified rectangular-wafer technology with any side at least 182 mm; or listed cutting technology for wafers at most 130 micrometers thick. The thin-wafer branch has additional wire or machine tests. CN-A1

39. High-efficiency TOPCon and back-contact cells¶

263802X / 1 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Six specified process groups cover coatings, diffusion and electrodes. Several combine 182 to 230 mm wafers with thickness at most 140 micrometers and stated production rates. The high-efficiency definition also applies. CN-A1

40. Heterojunction cell processes¶

263802X / 2 | Solar energy | Proposed restricted

Four specified groups cover conductive films, silicon films, low-silver materials and copper electrodes. Some branches require wafers at most 110 micrometers thick and further output or material tests. Check the complete high-efficiency note. CN-A1
