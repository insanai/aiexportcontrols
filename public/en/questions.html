09. Questions we can ask now¶

Does this end downloads of Chinese open models?¶

The announcement does not say that. The draft excludes AI technology already public through open source or papers, and it offers facilitation for open-source exports of restricted AI technology. Treatment of a particular new release still depends on its facts and the implemented procedure. CN-A1, notes 1 and 2

Is using a Chinese chatbot an export of the model?¶

MOFCOM's answer on restricted large models distinguishes remote services from transferring model technology. That is a useful boundary, not a universal exemption for data, prohibited technologies or other activities bundled into a service. CNQA

Is buying an AI company abroad just a financial transaction?¶

The temporary registration list includes an overseas entity becoming the owner or actual controller of domestic technology. We must therefore ask about control of the technology as well as the corporate deal. The announcement does not say every foreign investment is prohibited. CN48, section III(2)

Can a solar machine or a drug license be treated as ordinary goods?¶

Specified solar equipment is expressly included when it carries covered technology. Biomedicine has specific ownership-transfer exclusions and licensing facilitation. We identify what actually changes hands before choosing a category. CN-A1, notes 3 and 4 and biomedicine notes

What should a useful evidence record contain?¶

Our suggested record names the technology version, relevant code and point, measured performance, public/private artifacts, data categories, transfer form, recipient, destination and dates. It attaches the original source and explains any exclusion relied on. The registration form lists its own required fields; our record is a reading aid, not a substitute form. CN-A2

What can still change?¶

The final catalogue, detailed procedures, authoritative interpretations and later US rulemaking can change the answer. This edition fixes its research date so that future readers can see its limits. We should recheck a live rule before using this book for a real decision.

An existing public model¶

Already public AI technology is excluded in the draft. Identify the exact artifact, release date and license. A related paper does not necessarily disclose private weights. CN-A1, note 1

A first open release¶

The draft offers automatic licensing or similar facilitation for open-source exports of restricted AI technology. A first release is different from an already public artifact. The implemented process still matters. CN-A1, note 2

Private weights for a partner¶

Check the exact technology and alternative thresholds in 266504X. Overseas licensing can trigger current registration; do not assume a software license is government permission. CN48 CN-A1

A hosted model service¶

The official answer distinguishes API, web, apps, SaaS and lightweight local deployment for restricted large models. It is not a universal exception for prohibited core-data technology or other laws. CNQA

A change of ownership¶

Registration includes a domestic technology owner or actual controller becoming an overseas entity. Examine control of the technology, not just the physical movement of files. CN48, III(2)

A solar production line¶

Check each listed equipment category and the technology indicators, plus software, installation and training. Covered key equipment is included in temporary registration. CN-A1, note 4 CNQA
